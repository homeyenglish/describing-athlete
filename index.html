Describing Athletes: Physical vs Personality

Dalam teks deskriptif bertema atlet, terdapat dua dimensi utama: Physical Appearance (fitur jasmani luar yang terlihat kasatmata) dan Personality Characteristics (karakter mental, daya juang, dan watak perilaku).

👀 Physical Appearance (Ciri Fisik)

Menjawab pertanyaan: "What does he/she look like?"
Pola kalimat utama:
• Subject + is + Adjective (e.g. He is tall and athletic.)
• Subject + has + Adjective + Noun (e.g. She has tan skin and straight hair.)

🧠 Personality Characteristics (Karakter & Watak)

Menjawab pertanyaan: "What is he/she like?"
Pola kalimat utama:
• Subject + is + Adjective (e.g. Anthony is humble and hardworking.)
• Subject + always + Verb / behaves + Adverb (e.g. He always plays with a sporting attitude.)

Paragraph 1: Identification

Anthony Sinisuka Ginting is a world-famous Indonesian badminton player. He won a bronze medal at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics. Millions of badminton lovers admire him because he plays with high speed and lightning footwork on the court.

Paragraph 2: Physical Appearance

Anthony is a medium-height athlete with a very lean, athletic, and fit body. He has natural tan skin that often looks flushed and sweaty during intense rallies. His hair is short, straight, and cut neat. He has an oval face with sharp eyes, but he always shows a warm and bright smile to supporters.

Paragraph 3: Personality Characteristics

Off the court, Anthony is well-known as a humble person. During intense matches, he remains exceptionally calm and focused. He is extremely hardworking and disciplined. When he is trailing behind on score, he is resilient and determined to fight back. He always treats opponents with great sporting spirit.

Paragraph 1: Identification

Greysia Polii is a legendary Indonesian badminton champion. Together with Apriyani Rahayu, she made history by winning the prestigious Olympic Gold Medal in women's doubles at Tokyo 2020.

Paragraph 2: Physical Appearance

Greysia has a petite yet remarkably strong, athletic, and muscular body built through decades of training. She has a healthy fair skin tone. When playing, her long hair is tightly tied into a ponytail. She has an inviting round face with an iconic bright and warm smile.

Paragraph 3: Personality Characteristics

Greysia is admired for her cheerful and energetic personality. On court, she is exceptionally cooperative with her partner. Even when recovering from severe injuries, she remained extremely patient, brave, and resilient. Her passionate heart and humble attitude make her a true sports legend.
