Describing Athletes: Physical vs Character

Dalam teks deskriptif, terdapat dua aspek utama: Physical Appearance (ciri fisik yang terlihat mata) dan Personality Characteristics (sifat dan watak atlet).

👀 Physical Appearance (Ciri Fisik)

Menjawab pertanyaan: "What does he/she look like?"
Pola dasar: Subject + is + Adjective (e.g., He is tall) atau Subject + has + Adjective + Noun (e.g., He has tan skin).

🧠 Personality Characteristics (Karakter & Sifat)

Menjawab pertanyaan: "What is he/she like?"
Pola dasar: Subject + is + Adjective (e.g., She is hardworking and humble).

Paragraph 1: Identification

Anthony Sinisuka Ginting is a world-famous Indonesian badminton player. He won a bronze medal at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics. People love to watch him because he is very quick on the court.

Paragraph 2: Physical Appearance

Anthony is a medium-height athlete with a very lean, athletic, and fit body. He has natural tan skin that often looks flushed and sweaty during intense rallies. His hair is short, straight, and cut neat. He has an oval face with sharp eyes, but he always shows a warm and bright smile to fans.

Paragraph 3: Personality Characteristics

Off the court, Anthony is well-known as a humble person. During intense matches, he remains exceptionally calm and focused. He is extremely hardworking and disciplined. When he is trailing behind on score, he is resilient and determined to fight back. He always treats opponents with great sporting spirit.

Paragraph 1: Identification

Greysia Polii is a legendary Indonesian badminton doubles champion. Together with Apriyani Rahayu, she created history by winning the Olympic Gold Medal at Tokyo 2020.

Paragraph 2: Physical Appearance

Greysia has a petite yet remarkably strong, athletic, and muscular body built through years of dedication. She has a glowing fair skin tone. When playing, her long hair is tightly tied into a pony tail. She has a friendly round face with an iconic bright and warm smile.

Paragraph 3: Personality Characteristics

Greysia is loved for her cheerful and energetic personality. On the court, she is exceptionally cooperative with her partner. Even when recovering from injuries, she was extremely patient, brave, and resilient. Her passionate heart and humble attitude make her an inspiring role model.
